In short: You can use Travel Planner without an account; your trip data then stays only on your device. If you create an account, your data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) so it can be synced across your devices and shared with people you choose. The app has no ads, no analytics, no advertising ID and no location tracking. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

2. Two ways to use the app

Guest mode (no account): everything you enter — trips, expenses, to-dos, places and attached documents — is stored only in the app’s storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server, and we cannot see it.

With an account: when you sign in with e-mail or Google, your data stays on your device and is also stored on our servers so it can be synced, backed up and shared with trip members you invite. On first sign-in you are asked whether to move existing guest trips to your account; if you choose to keep them on the device, they are not uploaded.

5. AI trip planning

AI planning is optional and requires an account. On first use you are asked for your explicit consent to the transfer abroad; you can withdraw it at any time under Profile → Legal and privacy → Withdraw my consent.

  • What is sent: when you tap “Create plan”, the destination city and country, start date and number of days, your interests, pace, budget level, who you travel with, your optional note and the names of places already saved in the trip go to our plan function and from there to the AI provider OpenAI (USA).
  • What is not sent: your name, e-mail address, expenses and documents.
  • Checking: suggested places are verified on our server with mapping services that use OpenStreetMap data (Photon, Overpass).
  • Storage: the plan is prepared in the background and kept on our server only until it reaches your app, and in any case deleted within 1 day. Finished plans are kept only on your phone (the last 5 per trip) until the trip is deleted, so you can open them again.
  • Plan limit: to apply the limit we keep your account ID, an irreversible hash (SHA-256) derived from your device and a usage count. A summary of each request (time, outcome, duration, model; not the plan or what you typed) is kept for 1 year.

Please do not put personal or sensitive information in the note. AI suggestions can be wrong; check opening hours, prices and bookings. You can report an inappropriate suggestion with the “Report” link on the plan screen.

AI planning (OpenAI, USA), bot protection (Cloudflare, USA), Google sign-in, e-mail delivery and the other services above may process data on servers outside your country. Where appropriate safeguards are not possible, these transfers rely on the explicit consent you give when using the feature; AI planning and Google sign-in are entirely optional. For EEA users, data is hosted within the EU, and transfers to providers outside the EU rely on the safeguards of Chapter V GDPR (adequacy decisions and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses).

8. Retention

  • Guest data: on your device only, until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account data and synced content: as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted trips and items: kept in the in-app trash for 30 days so you can restore them. When that time ends, or when you delete them permanently, they are removed from the device; the deleted copy on the server is purged automatically within 30 days, and the cloud file of a deleted document within 31 days.
  • Invite codes: valid for 48 hours by default; deleted 30 days after they expire or are revoked.
  • AI plans: on the server only until the plan reaches your app, at most 1 day; finished plans on your phone only (last 5 per trip) until the trip is deleted.
  • When you delete your account: your account, profile and the trips you own (with their content and files) are deleted. For each trip you share with others you first choose to hand it over to another member (it stays with them) or to delete it for everyone; copies in the infrastructure provider’s routine backups expire within at most 30 days. Files uploaded to those trips are deleted from the cloud at once, or within 2 days if there is a connection problem. Content you added to other people’s trips stays there as part of those trips but is no longer linked to your account.
  • Action log: admin actions on your account (e.g. a ban or sign-out), the account deletion record (your account’s e-mail and ID, the date, who deleted it, device/browser details and IP address) and a summary of your AI plan requests (time, outcome, duration, model used; not the plan or what you typed) are kept for 1 year for security and accountability, then deleted automatically. This record stays for 1 year after you delete your account.
  • Device hash and accounts on the same device: 1 year after the device was last seen, then deleted automatically. It stays with the account’s e-mail for that period even if you delete your account.
  • Technical logs: for a limited period according to the providers’ log policies, or longer where the law requires.
  • Support and data-request correspondence: for the applicable statutory limitation periods.

When you sign out, you choose: with "Sign out" a copy of your account data remains on the device so it opens quickly next time; with "Sign out and delete my data from this device" that copy (database, documents and photos) is removed from the device - your data in the cloud is not deleted. The app warns you first if some changes haven't been uploaded yet. You can also remove the copy by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in system settings.

9. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Row-level security rules ensure only members of a trip can access it, within the limits of their role. Files are kept in a private storage bucket. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, session tokens in the operating system’s secure storage, and the provider encrypts data at rest. Data is not end-to-end encrypted, so as system administrator we could technically access it; we only do so at your request, to keep the service secure, or where legally required. If we become aware of a personal data breach, we will notify you and the competent authority as required by law.
