In short: You can use Travel Planner without an account; your trip data then stays only on your device. If you create an account, your data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) so it can be synced across your devices and shared with people you choose. The app has no ads, no analytics, no advertising ID and no location tracking. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy covers personal data processed through the Travel Planner mobile app (Android package com.eaksoft.travelplanner, including any future iOS version) and this website. The data controller is the app’s independent developer, Emrullah Akkuş (“we”, “us”). Contact: emrullahakkus9@gmail.com.

This policy is written to comply with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”) and, for users in the European Economic Area, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”). The Turkish version of this policy is the governing version.

2. Two ways to use the app

Guest mode (no account): everything you enter — trips, expenses, to-dos, places and attached documents — is stored only in the app’s storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server, and we cannot see it.

With an account: when you sign in with e-mail or Google, your data stays on your device and is also stored on our servers so it can be synced, backed up and shared with trip members you invite. On first sign-in you are asked whether to move existing guest trips to your account; if you choose to keep them on the device, they are not uploaded.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Creating your account, signing you in, sending verification and password-reset codes — performance of a contract (KVKK 5/2-c; GDPR 6(1)(b)).
  • Storing and syncing your trip data, keeping deleted items restorable for 30 days, trip sharing and role management — performance of a contract.
  • Providing weather, place search, maps and exchange rates — performance of a contract.
  • Keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, fixing errors — legitimate interests (KVKK 5/2-f; GDPR 6(1)(f)).
  • Answering requests and complying with legal obligations — legal obligation (KVKK 5/2-ç; GDPR 6(1)(c)); establishing, exercising or defending legal claims (KVKK 5/2-e; GDPR 6(1)(f)).

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, automated decision-making or advertising, and we do not send marketing e-mails.

6. International transfers

For account users, data is stored in Supabase’s data centre in Frankfurt, Germany (EU); for users in Türkiye this is a transfer abroad, carried out in accordance with Article 9 of the KVKK. Google sign-in, e-mail delivery and the other services above may process data on servers outside your country. For EEA users, data is hosted within the EU, and transfers to providers outside the EU rely on the safeguards of Chapter V GDPR (adequacy decisions and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses).

7. Retention

  • Guest data: on your device only, until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account data and synced content: as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted trips and items: kept in the in-app trash for 30 days so you can restore them, then permanently deleted from the device and the server.
  • Invite codes: deleted 30 days after they expire (default validity 7 days) or are revoked.
  • When you delete your account: your account, profile and the trips you own (with their content and files) are deleted. For each trip you share with others you first choose to hand it over to another member (it stays with them) or to delete it for everyone; copies in the infrastructure provider’s routine backups expire within at most 30 days.
  • Technical logs: for a limited period according to the providers’ log policies, or longer where the law requires.
  • Support and data-request correspondence: for the applicable statutory limitation periods.

When you sign out, you choose: with "Sign out" a copy of your account data remains on the device so it opens quickly next time; with "Sign out and delete my data from this device" that copy (database, documents and photos) is removed from the device - your data in the cloud is not deleted. The app warns you first if some changes haven't been uploaded yet. You can also remove the copy by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in system settings.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Row-level security rules ensure only members of a trip can access it, within the limits of their role. Files are kept in a private storage bucket. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, session tokens in the operating system’s secure storage, and the provider encrypts data at rest. Data is not end-to-end encrypted, so as system administrator we could technically access it; we only do so at your request, to keep the service secure, or where legally required. If we become aware of a personal data breach, we will notify you and the competent authority as required by law.

9. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK and, for EEA users, the GDPR, you can request access to your data, information about its processing and recipients, correction, deletion, notification of corrections or deletions to recipients, objection to decisions based solely on automated processing, and compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. EEA users can also request data portability and restriction, and object to processing based on legitimate interests.

You can view, edit, delete and export all your data (Profile → Data) inside the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page, or e-mail emrullahakkus9@gmail.com, preferably from the address registered in the app. We respond within 30 days, free of charge. You may lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or, in the EEA, with your local supervisory authority.

10. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: weather, maps, rates and sync; detecting when you are offline.
  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo for an attachment.
  • Photos / files: only when you pick a file; on Android 13+ the system photo picker is used.
  • Notifications, exact alarms, run at start-up, wake lock, vibration: trip reminders and budget alerts, scheduled on the device without a push server, and restored after a reboot.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 18, get permission from a parent or guardian before creating an account. Parents can contact us to have a child’s data deleted.

12. Information about other people

When you add participant names or documents containing other people’s data, you are responsible for informing them and, where necessary, obtaining their consent. People named in a trip who do not use the app may also contact us to exercise their rights.

13. This website

This website uses no cookies, no local storage, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts; all files are served from our own domain. The hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (IP address, browser, page, time) to operate the site securely. The data-request form does not send anything to a server; it only prepares an e-mail draft in your own mail app.

14. Changes

We may update this policy as the app or the law changes. The current version is always published on this page with its “Last updated” date. Material changes will be announced in the app or on this website.
